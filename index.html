How batch ordering works

Review the 4-step schedule before submitting your pre-order. Grouping orders weekly keeps bundle pricing low.

Batch Cut-Off: Every Friday · 10:00 PM

STEP 01

Submit your order

Complete the Google Form Monday to Friday before the 10 PM cut-off and follow the stated deposit instructions.

STEP 02

Friday 10 PM cut-off

Your CM sizes, selected colorways, and deposit details are verified and locked into the weekly batch.

STEP 03

Supplier processing

The consolidated batch is pulled from the supplier warehouse. Processing timelines depend on stock availability.

STEP 04

Check & dispatch

Every pair is checked before dispatch. Tracking or courier details are shared once ready to ship.

Before you send payment

Always confirm final price, exact CM size, product grade, payment terms, delivery coverage, and estimated arrival date with our page.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I mix different shoe sizes inside a Bundle or Mega Box? +

Yes! You can combine different sizes and colorways in a 2-Piece, 3-Piece, or 4-in-1 Mega Box—perfect for sharing a bundle with family, your partner, or friends. Just specify the exact CM size for each pair when ordering.

Why do you recommend measuring in Centimeters (CM)? +

US and EU sizing can vary slightly between sneaker models and slides. Measuring your foot length in CM (from heel to longest toe) or checking the `CM / JP` label inside your best-fitting sneaker ensures the most accurate fit.

What if my chosen colorway is out of stock when the batch closes? +

We recommend listing a 2nd-choice colorway on your order form. If both options are unavailable from the supplier, we will immediately notify you so you can swap models or request a refund of your deposit.
