LKIXX was created in loving memory of a cherished family member whose life ended too soon. The “L” carries her legacy, while “kixx” reflects our love for footwear and the everyday journeys that move us forward.

How batch ordering works

Review the steps before submitting your order. Availability and delivery timing can vary by batch.

STEP 01

Submit your order

Complete the Google Form Monday to Friday before the 10 PM cut-off and follow the stated deposit instructions.

STEP 02

Friday cut-off

Order details are checked and grouped into the weekly batch.

STEP 03

Supplier processing

The batch is submitted to the supplier. Processing and dispatch depend on supplier availability.

STEP 04

Check & delivery

Orders are checked before dispatch. Delivery details are shared once confirmed.

Before you pay

Confirm the final price, size, product grade, payment terms, delivery coverage, and estimated arrival date.
